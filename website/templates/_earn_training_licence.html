How your work is used

  • The rankings, ratings, fact-checks and written responses you submit are used to train and evaluate AI models, and are licensed to Avants AI's customers as datasets.
  • Datasets contain your responses only - never your name, email, resume or profile. Don't put personal information (yours or anyone else's) in written responses.
  • You're paid per task regardless. You can stop at any time, and we won't include responses from contributors who haven't accepted this licence.
  • Data already delivered to a customer can't be recalled, but it stops being included in future datasets once you stop contributing.
